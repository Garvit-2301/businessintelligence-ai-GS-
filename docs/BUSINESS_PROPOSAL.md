# Lithub — Business Proposal

Accenture Innovation Challenge 2026 · Round 2  
Team Lithub · IIT Patna · Garvit Sahni, Pranav Gupta, Jatin Aggarwal

## Problem

Operators are not short of charts. They are short of a close they can defend: a material movement, a ranked set of drivers, a persona-specific next step, and the discipline to abstain when two source systems disagree. Dashboards hide grain mismatches, stale official series, and launch KPIs with two weeks of history.

## Solution

Lithub is the Round 1 research-backed analyst loop, now running as a close.

1. Forecasting (Holt linear) detects movement against an expected weekly path.
2. KAHAN hierarchical reasoning attributes the parent miss down region → category → SKU and boosts nodes the knowledge graph already links to an event.
3. Causal attribution (difference-in-differences) isolates West hoodie from a Central+East control that shared the national price test.
4. Knowledge-graph walks retrieve event → SKU → lever → owner. They are not prompt memory.
5. Deterministic reconciliation, materiality, PVM identity, abstention, RBAC, and the action catalog sit around that loop (Round 2 constraints).
6. AI storytelling writes CFO / Category / Growth briefs over a frozen evidence pack. The model cannot invent a number.
7. Analyst feedback reweights drivers and reroutes owners.

## Users

- CFO / FP&A: materiality, cash gates, board language, explicit non-claims.
- Category: SKU-region levers, promo depth, replenishment.
- Growth: paid allocation only after definitions agree; suppress demand we cannot fill.

## Case

At Northline’s illustrated scale, a wrong weekly media reallocation on contested CAC is five figures. Advertising an unfilled hero SKU wastes the same. Judging a wearable launch on 18 days is a strategy error. Token cost of the brief is sub-cent. The product’s return is avoided bad meetings and hours of analyst reconstruction.

## Roadmap

1. Pilot close — five KPIs, three sources, three seats (this prototype).
2. Governed production — warehouse jobs, IdP security, labeled-week evaluation.
3. Causal and forecast priors — DiD on regional tests; hierarchical forecasts as the prior, not the storyteller.
4. Action fabric — tickets in the systems owners already use; monitoring that closes itself.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Hallucinated numbers | LLM never originates quantities; grounded Q&A refuses out-of-pack asks |
| Definition drift | Official vs proxy is first-class; contradiction → abstain |
| Sparse history | Confidence cap; no annualization |
| Entitlement leak | Mask before prompt |
| Cost | Small model, cached evidence |
| Feedback poisoning | Visible, resettable weights; change control in production |

## Native vs custom

The prototype is custom-built. Warehouse platforms would be configured for jobs and semantics; identity and tickets would be integrated. The reasoning plane — abstention, decision rights, evidence-locked narrative — stays custom because no dashboard product currently owns that contract.
